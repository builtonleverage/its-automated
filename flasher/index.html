Install It's Automated

Flash the open-source WiZ lamp bridge onto an ESP32 straight from this page. Needs Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop (phones can't flash over USB).

  1. Plug the ESP32 into your computer with a micro-USB data cable.
  2. Click Install and pick the serial port (usually "CP2102" or "USB to UART").
  3. If it can't connect, hold the BOOT button on the board while it starts.

After flashing: on your phone, join the Wi-Fi hotspot Its-Automated-Setup, choose your home Wi-Fi, then open http://its-automated.local.

Source and full instructions: github.com/builtonleverage/its-automated
